  ## Checks with multiple values and records

  - A manufacturing check can ask for several values, each with its own type: **Number**, **Yes/no**, **Text**, **Photo** or **File**. For example, a check per barrel can ask for pH, acidity and salt level. A check without values is a confirmation that it was done.
  - Each time a check is done, a record keeps its values. In **Number of records**, set how many are required. In the recipe, you can use a formula, such as `ceil(Production order quantity / 500)`. With **Allow adding more**, that number is a minimum and the operator can add more records.
  - **When is it required?** replaces the check's timing. **Before starting** blocks starting and finishing the operation, **Before finishing** blocks finishing, and **Optional** does not block it. Recording units does not depend on checks.
  - You can save a partial record and complete it later, for example when you have a test result. If two people complete different values of the same record, both are kept.
  - In work mode, checks appear in their order, with an icon showing when they are required. A check with a single record is filled in on its own card. A check with several records shows the list and **Add record**, and each record can have a reference, such as the barrel number.
  - Reorder checks with the arrows in the recipe and in the MO. From MO management, you can also add, edit and remove records.
  - Existing checks are converted automatically and keep the results already recorded. Checks at the start are required before starting, and checks at the end before finishing. In recipes, per-unit checks ask for as many records as they generated before.
  - Importing recipe checks in **Control panel > Import data** includes the value to record, its type, the number of records and when it is required.
  - In the API, the records of a check are read, created, updated and removed individually, and each photo or file is uploaded to its value. If your integration creates or completes checks, update it.

  ## Improvements

  - When you link WhatsApp in **Control panel > My account**, **Link from another device** shows a QR code. Scan it with the phone where you use WhatsApp to open it with the link message. You can also copy the number and the link code and send the code by hand before it expires.
  - Recipe formulas support `min` and `max` with two values, for example to cap a result with `min(100, ceil(Production order quantity / 500))`.
  - When you connect an assistant through MCP, Bold sends it usage instructions. They tell it to rely on Bold's results and documentation, not to invent data or rules, and not to assume a change succeeded unless Bold confirms it.

  ## Fixes

  - You can attach XML files to Boldy tasks, including when you send them through Telegram. Previously, Bold rejected the upload.
  - If you open an item without a description and leave without making changes, Bold no longer asks you to discard unsaved changes.
  - In an item's **Expected outgoing** and in the purchase and production assistants, the status filter offers planning statuses, such as **Not planned** or **Planned**.
  - In a lot's **Movement history**, co-product production and its reversal link to the MO or operation that caused them.
</Update>

  ## Boldy on WhatsApp

  - You can chat with Boldy from WhatsApp, through the Bold Factory number. In **Control panel > My account**, click **Link WhatsApp** in the **WhatsApp** section, then **Open WhatsApp**, and send the prewritten message without changing it. When Boldy confirms the link, the section shows **Linked as** followed by your number or profile name.
  - Each WhatsApp account belongs to a single user and can only be linked in one company at a time. To use it in another company, click **Unlink** in the current company and link it in the new one.
  - By default, a message sent within two hours of the previous one continues the most recent task. After that, a new message starts another task. To continue a specific task, reply by quoting a Boldy message from that task.
  - For now, Boldy only reads text messages on WhatsApp. If you send images, documents or voice notes, it tells you that it can only read text.
  - Tasks started from WhatsApp appear in **Smart Factory > Tasks** with their channel. You can view their activity and result, but replies are sent from WhatsApp. If Boldy finishes more than 24 hours after your last message, check the result in **Tasks**.

  ## Boldy conversation context

  - Next to a task's message box, the **context** indicator shows how much of Boldy's memory the conversation takes up. Hover over it to see the tokens used, the available maximum, the percentage at which Boldy summarizes the conversation, and an approximate split by category.
  - When the conversation approaches the maximum, Boldy summarizes it automatically and continues from the summary. This lets you keep working on long tasks.
  - A **History summarized** line marks that point in the task and in the chat panel. Boldy keeps your instructions and the most recent messages, but it can lose details from older messages. If earlier information matters, mention it again.

  ## Real-time workstation status

  - **Smart Factory > Dashboards > Workstation status** starts with a summary of active workstations out of the total, operations in progress, employees clocked in, and operations with alerts.
  - Workstations are grouped by area. Workstations without activity are dimmed and labeled **No activity**, so you can see at a glance which ones are idle.
  - Each operation shows its progress with a bar and the clocked-in employees with the time elapsed. Hover over an employee to see their name and start date.
  - The view highlights clock-ins open for more than 12 hours, in red after 24, operations whose target date has passed, and operations with fewer employees clocked in than they require.
  - The page refreshes every 30 seconds and shows when it was last updated.

  ## Improvements

  - When you create or edit a pause reason, choose the **Interruption type**: **Planned** or **Unplanned**. The form explains how each option affects the workstation's availability and OEE.
  - The **Effectiveness by employee** dashboard is now called **Productivity by employee**.
  - **Production > Resources > Diagram** shows each area as a card containing its workstations and nested sub-areas. Every workstation appears inside its area. A single click opens the area or workstation, and workstations with infinite capacity are labeled as such.
  - In MO and operation management, all actions are in a single bottom action bar, as in the rest of the application. Order or operation actions are always visible, alongside those of the open tab, such as saving or adding a material, an assignment or a restriction.
  - You can no longer change the item of an MO input or co-product. To use another item, delete the input or co-product and add a new one. This way, material planning, stock reservations and planned cost are linked to the correct item. The API also rejects this change.
  - In the `Items.Sku.Deleted` webhook, the `mergedIntoSkuId` field identifies the surviving item when the deletion is part of a consolidation.

  ## Fixes

  - When items are consolidated, sales order lines in **Unconfirmed draft** or **Pending** status with no deliveries move to the surviving item and keep their status instead of being cancelled. Manufacturing requests with no assigned quantity also always move to the surviving item.
  - A timed wait that you pause, or whose completion you undo, stays paused until you resume it manually. Previously, it could resume on its own.
  - If an operation started before its start condition was met, the restriction shows as unmet even after the preceding operation finishes. The shortcut to the preceding operation only appears while work mode lists it.
  - Employee pictures, item images and images embedded in formatted text display correctly.
  - **Employee activity** shows each employee's picture.
  - In Boldy tasks, your messages display their formatting, such as lists, bold text or links.
  - Boldy takes the company's configured language into account instead of assuming Spanish.
</Update>

  ## Restrictions between operations

  - When you connect two operations in a recipe or an MO, choose **Full · wait until finished**, **Partial · advance as units are completed** or **Suggested order - do not block**. With **Custom**, you configure separately when the next operation can start and how many units can be completed in it.
  - Each condition defines how it applies. The start condition can warn but allow, be hidden by default but allowed after a warning, or be hidden and forbidden. The unit limit can warn but allow, or forbid.
  - Existing restrictions are converted to the new conditions. Those that do not match a predefined option open in **Custom** with their values.
  - In the operations diagram, drag the port of one operation onto another to connect them. Hover over an arrow to see its restriction and right-click it to edit or delete it. **Recalculate positions** places each operation below its predecessors. Double-clicking opens an operation in read-only diagrams too.
  - In work mode, a forbidding condition prevents you from starting the operation or recording more units, and Bold shows the reason. If the condition only warns, Bold asks you to confirm before continuing. **Hide restricted** only hides operations that have not started yet.
  - In MO management, you can skip a forbidding restriction if you have write permission for manufacturing orders. Bold asks you to confirm before continuing.
  - Production plans take each restriction's start and unit conditions into account, even when they only warn. Operation group actions do not apply individual restrictions.
  - In the API, restrictions replace the `type` field with `start` and `quantity`. If your integration creates or reads restrictions, update it.

  ## Shared progress recording in work mode

  - The quantities, consumptions, lots and co-products you prepare when recording progress are saved automatically. If you reload the page, switch screens or continue on another tablet, you recover what you had prepared.
  - Each operation has a single draft, shared by every employee and device working on it. Lines changed by someone else show **Changed by** and their name, or **Changed on another device**.
  - If the theoretical quantity of a line changed after you edited it, Bold warns you before you confirm. If another device has already confirmed or discarded the record, Bold shows the updated pending quantities instead of recording them twice.
  - When offline, Bold indicates that it will save the changes once the connection is back. The browser warns you if you try to leave with unsaved changes.
  - The draft is discarded when you record the progress and when you finish or cancel the operation.

  ## Application in Spanish and English

  - The application is available in Spanish and English, including validation messages, errors and contextual help.
  - Choose your **Language** in **Control panel > My account > Preferences**. **Company language** uses the language set in **Control panel > Settings**. Your preference applies to all your companies.
  - Each employee can have their own **Language** for work mode. When it is not set, the company language applies.
  - Numbers and dates follow the company format, whatever language you choose.

  ## Effectiveness by employee

  - **Smart Factory > Dashboards > Effectiveness by employee** compares an employee's recorded work with their schedule over the period you choose.
  - A timeline distinguishes time worked inside and outside the schedule, idle time, including idle time after a stop, and non-working days or days without a defined schedule.
  - The indicators are **Utilization**, **Efficiency**, **Quality**, **Productivity over schedule** and **OLE**, the equivalent of OEE for people. You can also review the breakdown by day and by operation.
  - Leave and holidays recorded as work days without periods in the employee's personal schedule do not count as idle time.

  ## Stock at date

  - **Warehouse > Stock control > Stock history** adds **Stock at date**. It shows the stock of each item, location and lot at a past date and time, with shortcuts for common closing dates such as the end of the previous month or year.
  - You can limit the query to an item or a location, use **Sum lots** or **Sum locations** and show zero combinations with **Include records without stock**. Negative stock is highlighted in red.
  - Quantities are those at that moment, but codes and names are the current ones. **Export** downloads the result in Excel or CSV.
  - The API lets you query and export stock at a date.

  ## Purchases reserved for an MO

  - An MO's purchase assistant shows all its purchased materials, including when they are already covered. The button reads **Resolve shortages** when material is missing and **Purchase assistant** otherwise.
  - **Propose what the production order needs** fills in the quantity the order needs for one line or for all of them at once.
  - Purchase orders created from the assistant are reserved for that MO. They cover its needs first, and only the surplus is available for other needs. Once received, the material becomes regular stock.

  ## Improvements

  - In **Control panel > My account**, you can change your first and last name. Your email identifies your account and cannot be changed. The linked employee's name does not change.
  - From MO management, you can start, pause, resume and undo the finish of a wait. Pauses delay its automatic completion.
  - A wait starts automatically when the start conditions of its restrictions are met. It does not finish while any of its preceding operations remains open.
  - Notifications, webhooks and automations include the event for the start of a manufacturing operation.
  - **Shipments** shows the **Cancelled** status. The **Hide cancelled** filter, enabled by default, hides cancelled shipments, including in a customer's shipments. A sales order's shipment lines no longer show cancelled shipments.

  ## Fixes

  - When you choose or scan a lot in work mode, Bold uses the stock at the operation's input location (**Input stock**) instead of the total across all locations.
  - Automations triggered by an event receive that event's data.
  - Automation instructions accept up to 30,000 characters instead of being limited to 12,000.
  - The **Load planner** shows the customer name instead of its identifier, and each line's required date in **Required**.
  - Long filters in a notification subscription wrap to the dialog width instead of overflowing it.
  - The **Stock forecast** chart in the purchase assistant shows negative stock with its sign in the tooltip.
  - To-order needs are served in order of their needed date when stock and expected receipts are allocated.
</Update>

  ## Attended operations, unattended operations and waits

  - Each process has an **Activity type**: **Process**, for productive work, or **Wait**, for periods such as resting or drying.
  - Choose **Staff attendance** for each station compatible with a process. **Attended** requires personnel presence, and **Unattended** lets the machine work on its own. You can change this from the process, from the station's capabilities or when importing data.
  - Each operation takes its execution mode from the activity type and the selected station. When you change the configuration, Bold updates operations that have not started. Started and canceled operations keep their mode.
  - In work mode, an attended operation needs an employee to start or resume. An unattended operation does not, and you can stop it with **Stop** even when nobody is working on it. You cannot start a process without a selected station.
  - Waits start automatically when their preceding operations finish, run continuously for their planned duration and finish automatically. You cannot start them manually or pause them.
  - Unattended operations and waits do not reserve personnel in production plans.
  - Stations no longer have the **Complete operations automatically** option. Processes that completed this way become **Wait** activities.

  ## Operation comments

  - Operations support formatted comments and attachments. In MO management, view them in **Info**, below the order notes. In work mode, they have a separate **Comments** section and are attributed to the active employee.
  - The most recent comments appear first. You can comment on completed operations. Canceled operations display their comments but do not allow you to add or delete them.
  - Progress recording in work mode no longer includes a comments field. Use **Comments** to leave observations about the operation.
  - The API lets you view, add and delete an operation's comments.

  ## Improvements

  - **Control panel > Notifications** displays your active subscriptions as cards, with the entity, event, channel and applied filters. **Create a subscription** guides you through choosing a channel, entity, event and filters. Each card lets you **Edit filters** or **Remove** the subscription.
  - When editing a subscription's filters, Bold indicates whether a condition, an **AND** or **OR** operator or a parenthesis is missing. You cannot save the filter until it is complete.
  - Notifications and webhooks include two new purchase order events: a line was added and a line's cancellation was undone.
  - Operation cards in work mode show the employees working on them. Click or hover over their initials to see their full names.
  - In work mode, **Materials** shows whether each material has sufficient stock, is on its way, with the expected date when known, or is missing.
  - Work orders add the **Work** and **Finish** permissions. **Work** lets you complete actions, record time and materials and comment without editing the work order. Completing a work order requires **Finish**, so review which profiles should have it.

  ## Fixes

  - Receipts no longer accept draft purchase order lines. Bold prevents you from adding these lines or receiving a receipt that includes them.
  - Deleting a shipment releases its lines' external references. An integration can then send those lines again in another shipment.
</Update>

  ## Global search

  - Global search (**Ctrl+K**) brings records, application sections and documentation into one dialog. Only sections you can access appear, and documentation results open in another tab.
  - Results include movements, containers, load orders and warehouse counts. You can also find a record by its full identifier.

  ## Stock history

  - **Warehouse > Stock control > Stock history** shows stock changes for all items, with filters for item, location, lot and cause. Query periods of up to 365 days; the default shows the last 7 days. You can also include lot reclassifications.
  - The item's stock tab shows its recent changes. **View more** opens the history filtered by that item.
  - **Export data** adds **Stock history** in CSV or Excel format.

  ## Preventive maintenance and work orders

  - Preventive schedules support the **Weekdays** frequency, Monday to Friday, and filters for asset, cause and frequency. **Generate now** creates a work order immediately, and the **Generated orders** tab shows each schedule's work orders.
  - Work order actions can be required. You cannot complete the work order while required actions remain pending.
  - Each action separates instructions explaining how to perform it from the execution notes the technician adds when completing it.
  - Work orders include formatted **Comments** and attachments. You can add them from **Maintenance > Work orders** and from work mode.
  - An action's **Uses** tab shows which causes plan it and which work orders include it. Deleting an action removes it from open work orders where it is still pending and preserves it in the history of work orders where it was completed.

  ## Make to order and customers on manufacturing requests

  - Sales lines with **Pending** or **Partially delivered** status offer **Make to order**. The line changes to **Make to order** and, if the item is manufactured, Bold creates a manufacturing request linked to the order and its customer. The button is disabled when the line already has an active request.
  - Manufacturing requests can link to a customer, whose name is used as the requester. Without a customer, the requester is optional free text. Each MO copies the customer and requester from its request.
  - Existing requests and MOs linked to sales orders take the customer from the order. Generic **Manual**, **MRP** and **Stock** requester values are removed, so review recipe conditions that compare the requester against those values.

  ## Preferred manufacturing units

  - Each item can have a manufacturing unit and a warehouse unit, selected from its alternative units. Manufacturing work mode displays and records quantities in the manufacturing unit. Warehouse views continue to display the base unit.
  - Alternative unit equivalences use two quantities, such as `3 cajas = 1 palé`, and can be edited. This preserves relationships that cannot be expressed exactly in one direction. The API also accepts equivalences with both quantities.

  ## Formula and notes editor

  - Recipe formulas support the `round`, `ceil` and `floor` rounding functions from the **Round** action. The editor supports undo and redo with `Ctrl+Z` and `Ctrl+Y`, and operand suggestions open when you type `@`.
  - Notes on work orders, preventive schedules, purchase and sales orders, their lines and manufacturing requests support formatting, headings, links and attachments. Existing records display formatted notes; switch to editing with **Edit** or a double click.

  ## Features by plan

  - **Control panel > Subscription** shows storage usage and its limit alongside employees, SKUs and locations. Bold rejects file uploads that exceed the plan limit.
  - The **Features** section shows whether the plan includes advanced planning, receipt quality checks, alternative units of measure, multiple lot management, webhooks, advanced logistics and analytics reports.
  - When the plan excludes a feature, the application hides or disables its actions. For example, **Reports** indicates that analytics reports are not included. Previously configured alternative units and receipt checks remain visible.

  ## Improvements

  - **Import data** adds **Update cost** for items. Use a CSV or Excel file to update planned unit cost and whether cost updates automatically in bulk.
  - **Control panel > Files** identifies the record using each file, links to it and indicates whether Bold manages it.
  - Messages in tasks with Boldy and the additional **Run now** message in automations support up to 30,000 characters.
  - The **Medium** and **High** intelligence levels use a newer AI model.
  - The API reference includes a summary for every operation and groups analytics exports by business concept.

  ## Fixes

  - Theoretical manufacturing cost distributes the fixed time of recipe steps across the item's replenishment quantity, based on its minimum order or lot size, instead of charging all of it to each unit.
  - Automatic planned cost recalculation handles concurrent changes in several components of the same item better and no longer records changes when the estimate remains the same.
  - Unit conversions round results to the saved precision instead of rejecting quantities with too many decimal places.
  - Imports reject unrecognized values in yes/no columns instead of interpreting them as “no”.
  - Tasks with Boldy also allow retries when the AI provider is overloaded.
  - Long code blocks in Boldy's responses fit the available width instead of overflowing the conversation.
</Update>

  ## Complete operation groups

  - In work mode, operation groups offer **Confirm without completing** and **Confirm and complete**. When you complete the full pending quantity, Bold distributes it across operations according to the plan.
  - To complete a group before reaching its planned quantity, enter the final quantity for each operation. You can use zero or exceed the plan, but the breakdown must add up to the group's final total.

  ## Plan solving modes

  - When solving a production plan, or creating one with solving enabled, choose the **Solving mode**. **Basic (fast)** finds a feasible sequence using a heuristic. **Advanced (optimized)** refines that sequence to improve the result and can take several minutes.
  - The application initially selects **Basic**. If you integrate through the API and omit the mode, the server uses **Advanced**.

  ## Maintenance actions

  - Maintenance actions support two new result types: **Selection**, with options configured on the action, and **Signature**, drawn on screen.
  - You can **Undo** a completed action from **Maintenance > Work orders** or work mode. The action returns to planned status, its result and completing employee are cleared, and its notes remain. You cannot undo actions on completed or canceled work orders.

  ## Manufacturing order purchases

  - An MO's purchasing assistant shows all missing purchased materials in one table. The **Supplier** column suggests each item's main supplier and lets you select another of its suppliers.
  - Bold creates one purchase order per supplier at the same time, with the originating MO identified in its notes. You can then view the created orders in a dialog. Items without a supplier appear dimmed and are excluded.

  ## Improvements

  - **Manufacturing > Recipes** adds the **Hide inactive** quick filter.
  - Item and component costs display two decimal places; the tooltip retains four. Component links open their **Costs** tab directly.
  - Recipe inputs use an icon to indicate whether the material is a **Specific item** or based on properties.
  - The **Low**, **Medium** and **High** intelligence levels use new AI models.

  ## Fixes

  - When you leave a partial warehouse movement and return, you can continue from the recorded load and location, even if you skipped pickups.
  - During a movement, you can deliver picked material even if the linked need changed or disappeared. Bold reserves only the quantity still needed.
  - Planned movement actions are accepted even when you revisit the same location.
  - When preparing a load order with lines from multiple orders, each selected line is added only once.
  - A manufactured item's planned cost retains its last value while any recipe input cannot be resolved.
  - Tasks with Boldy are no longer marked complete when a response leaves a tool result pending.
</Update>
