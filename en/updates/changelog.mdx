<Update
  label="2026-09-14"
  tags={["New features", "Improvements", "Fixes"]}
>
  ## Planning assistants

  - The purchasing and production assistants let you work only with items procured to stock or include all items.

  ## More capabilities for Boldy

  - Boldy can query the official documentation and use more cost, catalog, maintenance, production and warehouse tools when the user and agent have the necessary permissions.

  ## Improvements

  - Alternative unit configuration explains the conversion factor more clearly, and item creation retains the selected unit.

  ## Fixes

  - Automations run with the permissions saved in their configuration, even if the permissions of the person starting the run have changed.
  - Voice messages sent from Telegram support more audio format variants.
  - Deleting a maintenance action also removes it from the causes where it was planned.
</Update>

<Update
  label="2026-09-07"
  tags={["New features", "Improvements", "Fixes"]}
>
  ## Alternative units of measure

  - The catalog adds unit of measure management. You can define a main unit, alternative units and their conversion factor for each item.
  - Sales and purchase orders let you enter quantities in an alternative unit. Bold retains the chosen unit and shows quantities with their unit in planning, production, warehouse and work mode.

  ## Theoretical manufacturing costs

  - The planned cost of manufactured items is calculated from materials, labor and machines. Item details separate direct costs from costs inherited from semi-finished items.

  ## Boldy by voice and Telegram

  - You can link your account from your profile to chat with Boldy in Telegram. Conversations support images, files and voice messages.
  - Smart Factory tasks let you record or attach audio. Bold transcribes the recording and displays the transcript next to the message.

  ## Improvements

  - Boldy's responses in Telegram retain Markdown formatting and display typing status while the response is prepared.
  - MCP connections can query Bold concept documentation as context resources.

  ## Fixes

  - When releasing an MO, property-based inputs select the family's only available item even if the recipe retains property values that no longer exist.
  - The cost breakdown correctly includes materials with **Ignore** procurement and keeps direct and inherited costs separate.
</Update>

<Update
  label="2026-08-31"
  tags={["New features", "Improvements", "Fixes"]}
>
  ## Subscription and AI credits

  - **Control panel > Subscription** brings together the main plan, purchased integrations, AI credits and invoices. The **Payment method** tab shows the current method and lets you change it through the secure Stripe form embedded in Bold. Administrators can also download invoices and complete payments that need intervention.
  - The payment method step in administrator registration uses the embedded Stripe form, keeping the user within the onboarding experience.
  - Credits included in the plan and purchased credits are shown separately. You can view monthly usage and purchase packs; the purchased balance carries over from one month to the next.
  - **Smart Factory > Tasks** warns when 20% or less of the available credits remain. Administrators can start a purchase from the warning itself.

  ## Assistants and integrations

  - You can connect ChatGPT Desktop or Claude Code to Bold through MCP and an API key to query operational information using natural language.
  - API lists standardize pagination, search, filters and sorting. The `Expand` parameter lets you explicitly request related data, including relationships up to two levels deep when the route supports them.

  ## Improvements

  - Recipe conditions correctly distinguish **Production order quantity** from **Requested quantity**, display labels in Spanish and validate the formula when you save it.

  ## Fixes

  - Receipts correctly display the order and item for pending purchase lines.
  - Old recipe version links lead to their current address, and warehouse movements retain their navigation path.
</Update>

  ## Tasks and automations

  - Tasks with Boldy allow retries when the AI provider temporarily has no capacity and explain when credits have run out. Conversations identify each message's author and display explanations of clarification options.

  ## Fixes

  - You can release an MO for an item that does not belong to a family.
  - To remove an operation's last active assignment, you must pause it first, preventing ongoing work from being left without an employee.
  - Tasks adapt better to mobile screens, and the load order calendar avoids scrollbars when you drag an order.
</Update>

<Update
  label="2026-07-27"
  tags={["New features", "Improvements", "Fixes"]}
>
  ## New features

  - When entering work mode, you can search for an employee if they do not appear in the list. When resuming, you choose **We all resume** or **Only me**.
  - **Change workstation** lets you apply the change only to this operation, to the immediately following operations or to all pending operations in the order.
  - You can **Cancel movement** when it is not yet transporting stock, specifying the reason.
  - Recipes allow you to **Copy operation to another recipe**, including inputs, steps and conditions.
  - From a manufacturing order, you can **Resolve with the purchase assistant**, view **Related orders** and adjust the **Target date**.
  - **Import data** accepts CSV or Excel and adds employees, locations, areas, workstations, operation types, maintenance assets, properties and item suppliers.
  - **Export data** adds item, manufacturing order and operation costs.
  - When preparing a load order, you can specify a partial **Quantity to load** and filter pending lines.
  - Work orders support **Undo finish**. Operations groups can include paused operations.

  ## Improvements

  - Sequencing brings together **Orders**, **Operations** and **Plans** views, with filters by area, workstation and tool, and the **Hide planned** option.
  - Resource assignment adds filters by order code, operation, requester, process, area and workstation.
  - When setting a property value for an item, only its applicable properties appear.
  - **What is this?** help links to the documentation through **More information**.

  ## Fixes

  - Pausing in work mode does not close assignments. You can change workstation even while the operation is running.
  - A manufacturing order's purchasing assistant discards previous proposals when the requirement changes.
  - If not all related orders can be loaded, the table indicates this instead of hiding the rest.
</Update>

  ## New features

  - Warehouse lets you plan load orders from pending sales orders or lines ready to ship, schedule them on a calendar and complete the work from work mode.

  - A manufacturing order's time records can be edited or deleted, including their assignments, employees, pauses, timestamps and comments.

  - Data export expands its catalog for items, people, planning, warehouse, production and maintenance, in CSV or Excel format.
  - Administrators can choose whether **My operations** uses employees' usual workstations or skills, and configure automatic load order provisioning.

  ## Improvements

  - Sales planning shows delivery instructions and expected shipment dates.
  - Employee indicator cards list each active operation and link to its details.
  - Agent sessions use the **Allow changes** and **Read only** controls to clarify whether the agent can perform actions.
  - Navigation groups warehouse tools by workflow, names the configuration area **Control panel**, and provides access to documentation from the header.
  - Options for stopping work are clearer, and the production filter uses the correct term for restricted operations.

  ## Fixes

  - When stopping work on a shared operation, you can choose to pause the whole team or let others continue. The system shows the appropriate pause reasons for shared workstations.
  - In-app and email notification toggles maintain independent saving states.
  - The Purchasing assistant creates the order and its lines in a single operation and discards the previous success state when the proposal changes.
  - Agent automations without a start date can be edited without errors.
</Update>
