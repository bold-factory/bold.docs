A **location** represents a physical or logical place where Bold records stock. It can be an entire warehouse, an area, an aisle, a rack, a bin, a production station or a temporary receiving area.

A location is more than a name. It defines where you expect to find material and where inflows, outflows, movements, stock counts and consumption are recorded.

## What it represents

| Type | Example | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General warehouse | `ALM-01` | Simple operations with little detail. |
| Area | `RECEPCION`, `PICKING`, `RESERVA` | Separate routes or physical areas. |
| Position | `A-03-02` | Find material precisely. |
| Shop floor | `LINEA-1`, `CORTE` | Consume or produce near the operation. |
| Temporary | `ENTRADA`, `SALIDA` | Record material during a temporary physical stage. |

<Info>
  A location's name does not block stock or change its availability. A separate location helps you know where material is, but any usage restriction must be controlled through the corresponding operational process.
</Info>

## When to use one location

A single location can be sufficient if:

- your team finds material without recording internal positions;
- you do not need stock counts by area;
- internal movements do not add traceability;
- the warehouse is small or has low volume;
- your priority is to start with a simple model.

This model reduces maintenance but provides less precision for searches and discrepancies.

## When to create several locations

Create several locations when the physical place changes operational decisions.

| Requirement | Useful locations |
| --- | --- |
| Receiving separated from available stock | `RECEPCION` and `ALMACEN` |
| Picking separated from reserve | `PICKING` and `RESERVA` |
| Production consumes from the shop floor | Locations by line, station or cell. |
| Stock counts by area | Locations matching counting areas. |

## Level of detail

A structure that is too detailed requires constant movement recording. A structure that is too broad makes discrepancies harder to find.

Design locations at the level your team works:

| If your team makes decisions by... | Model it as... |
| --- | --- |
| Entire warehouse | One location per warehouse. |
| Operational area | One location per area. |
| Exact bin | One location per position. |
| Consumption station | One location per station or line. |

## Use cases

| Case | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier receipt | First record it in a receiving location if it requires prior review. |
| Material ready for picking | Move it to an available or preparation location. |
| Production | Use shop floor locations if you need to know what material is next to each resource. |
| Stock count | Count locations that match the physical route. |
