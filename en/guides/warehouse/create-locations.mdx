**Warehouse locations** represent the physical places where Bold controls stock. You can use one location or detail warehouses, aisles, shelves, docks, and work areas.

**Path:** **Warehouse > Locations**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the location">
    Click **Create new warehouse location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name">
    Enter a clear, recognizable **Name**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the location">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

In the location details, you can view stock by item and lot. To correct a quantity, use an adjustment or stock count. To transfer materials, record a movement.
