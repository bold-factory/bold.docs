A movement transfers stock between locations without creating demand or supply.

**Path:** **Work mode > Warehouse > Movements**

## Execute a planned movement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select equipment">
    Choose the equipment you will use for the transfer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the movement">
    Open a movement in **Ready** status and click **Start**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the source">
    Click **I'm here** or scan the location code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record pick-ups">
    Confirm each pick-up with **Picked up**. Click **Continue** when finished at that location. If the equipment is full or you will not pick up more materials for this movement, use **I can't load any more**. The movement takes you to deliver what you are already carrying; pending pick-ups remain unexecuted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record deliveries">
    Confirm the destination and each delivery with **Left**. If the material need changes while you carry the load, deliver the picked-up materials anyway; the system updates their allocation. If you leave the movement and return, you can continue from the recorded load and location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the movement">
    Click **Complete movement** when the current load is empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a direct transfer

Click **New movement** to transfer materials without a planned task. Specify the source and confirm pick-up. Then select the destination and confirm delivery following the screen's instructions.

<Info>
  In management mode, open **Warehouse > Movements** to view transfer status and history.
</Info>
