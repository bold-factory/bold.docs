An MO is the work the shop floor needs to do. You create it from a **manufacturing request**, which states which item you need and how many units. See [Manufacturing requests](/en/concepts/manufacturing/manufacturing-requests) to see how they relate.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the request">
    Go to **Production > Production requests > Add new request**. Choose the item, enter a small quantity, for example `5`, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the MO">
    Click the rocket icon action, **Launch production order**. Check that the recipe you activated appears and confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  When you work with sales orders, Bold helps you decide what to manufacture from them. See [Sell](/en/guides/sales/sell).
</Note>
