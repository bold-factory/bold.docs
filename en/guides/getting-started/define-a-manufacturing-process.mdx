A **recipe** describes how an item is manufactured. Bold uses it as a template to create MOs. To start, one operation is enough. See [Recipes and versions](/en/concepts/manufacturing/recipes-and-versions) to learn its full structure.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the recipe">
    Go to **Production > Recipes** and add a recipe. Use the item's name as its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the item">
    In the recipe, click **Link item** and choose the item you created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a version">
    Add a version. Bold uses the date as its name.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an operation">
    Click **+ Add operation**, choose the process you created, and enter a name for the operation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the version">
    Click **Activate version**. New MOs use the active version.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Materials, inspections, and multiple operations can wait. Add them in a new version once you complete your first MO. See [Design the manufacturing steps](/en/guides/manufacturing/design-the-manufacturing-steps).
</Tip>
