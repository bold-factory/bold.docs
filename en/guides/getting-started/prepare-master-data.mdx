To manufacture your first MO, you need three things: a workstation where the work happens, a process performed there, and the item you will manufacture.

Bold creates a warehouse location named after your company and an employee so the person who signs up the account can record shop floor work.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an area and a workstation">
    Go to **Production > Resources**. Add an area, for example `Workshop`, and a workstation inside it, for example `Assembly bench 1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the process">
    Go to **Production > Processes > Add new type** and create the process, for example `Assembly`. Then open the process, click **+ Assign workstation**, and choose the workstation you created.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the item">
    Go to **Catalog > Items > Create new**. Enter the name, choose the unit, and select **Make** as the procurement source. You can leave the family empty.
  </Step>
</Steps>
