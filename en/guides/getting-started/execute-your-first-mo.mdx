**Work mode** is the screen the shop floor uses. From it, each person records what they do, at which workstation, and how many units they manufacture.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open work mode">
    Click **Work mode** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify yourself">
    Choose your employee. Bold links the work you record to that person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the operation">
    Go to **Production** and find your MO's operation by process or by workstation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the operation">
    Click **Start**. Bold starts tracking the working time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the units">
    Click **Complete** to open the record. Enter the units manufactured with **+** and **-**, or click **All** to record the full quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the operation">
    Click **Confirm and finish**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Review the MO

Click **Back to Management Mode** and open the MO in **Production > Production orders**.

- Its status is **Finished**.
- **Progress** shows the units you recorded.
- **Times** shows who worked on the operation and for how long.

<Check>
  You have manufactured your first MO from start to finish. The item's stock has increased by the units manufactured.
</Check>
