**Work mode** is the screen the shop floor uses. From it, each person records what they do, at which workstation, and how many units they manufacture.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open work mode">
    Click **Work mode** in the header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify yourself">
    Choose your employee. Bold links the work you record to that person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the operation">
    Go to **Production** and find your MO's operation by process or by workstation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the operation">
    Click **Start**. Bold starts tracking the working time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record the units">
    Enter the units manufactured with **+** and **-**, or click **All** to record the full quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the operation">
    Click **Complete**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Go back to the **Control panel** and open the MO. It is finished, and the item's stock has increased by the units manufactured.
</Check>

## If something doesn't work

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The operation doesn't appear | The recipe version is active and the MO was created after you activated it. |
| You can't start the operation | The workstation is assigned to the operation's process. |

To learn about pauses, defects, materials, and inspections, see [Execute shop floor operations](/en/guides/manufacturing/execute-shop-floor-operations).
