In this guide, you manufacture a product from start to finish: you prepare it, launch a manufacturing order (MO), and execute it from the shop floor. When you finish, Bold shows the MO as finished, the units manufactured, and the item's stock.

Choose a simple product your team knows. You don't need to configure anything else to start.

## Four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the workstation and the item">
    Create a workstation, the process performed there, and the item you will manufacture.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define how it is made">
    Create a recipe with a single operation and activate it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch the MO">
    Specify which item you need and how many units, and turn it into an MO.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manufacture on the shop floor">
    Open **work mode**, start the operation, record the units, and complete it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  When you complete the operation, the MO appears as finished in the **Control panel** and the item's stock increases by the units manufactured.
</Check>
