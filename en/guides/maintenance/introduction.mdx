Maintenance management brings together interventions on machines, equipment, tools, and facilities. Use it to plan inspections and retain the history of failures, time, and materials.

## Before you start

Decide how much detail you need to identify equipment and causes. Too much detail makes work harder to record; too little limits analysis of recurring problems.
