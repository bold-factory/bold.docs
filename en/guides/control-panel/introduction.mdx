## Recommended order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure the company">
    Review general data, time zone, language, activity and prefixes before creating records.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite users">
    Design permission profiles by responsibility and give the team access once the base configuration is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import or export data">
    Upload master data with consistent codes or download a copy for analysis and archiving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize files">
    Review attached documents and remove duplicates or obsolete files when appropriate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you start

Define who will administer the company and who can import data. Configuration changes, imports and file management can affect several modules.
