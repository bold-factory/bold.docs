The People module brings together employees and their schedules for manufacturing, maintenance and productivity analysis.

## Recommended order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register an employee">
    Register the people involved in production, maintenance or costs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure shifts and calendars">
    Create base work days, build shift patterns and record public holidays or closures by site.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign employee schedules">
    Apply the shift, local calendar and exceptions that correspond to each person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scheduling priority

An employee's effective schedule is resolved in this order:

1. Explicit assignment for a particular date.
2. Local calendar exception.
3. Repeating shift pattern.
