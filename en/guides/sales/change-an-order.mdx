Edit an order to correct its data or reflect a customer change without creating another document.

<Note>
  You can change a customer reference, correct an error or adjust a delivery date.
</Note>

## Edit the order

**Path:** **Planning > Sales orders > Order you want to edit**

From this screen, you can change:

- **Order no.**;
- **Customer order no.**;
- **Customer**;
- **Order date**;
- **Short description**;
- **Delivery instructions**;
- **Notes**.

Click **Save** after editing the header.

## Edit an order line

**Path:** **Planning > Sales orders > Order you want to edit > Item line**

To edit the requested, committed or target delivery dates, open the item line, make your changes and click **Save**.

## Change the dates of several lines

**Path:** **Planning > Sales orders > Order you want to edit**

Use this option when the customer changes the date of the whole order or of several lines at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the lines">
    In the **Lines** table, select the lines you want to change. If you don't select any, the change applies to every line that is not delivered or cancelled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the date change">
    Click **Change dates**. Each date shows its current value: the shared date, or the date range when the lines differ.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the dates that change">
    Check each date you want to edit and enter the new value. Unchecked dates keep their value on each line. If you check a requested or committed date and leave it empty, it is removed from the lines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the change">
    Click **Apply**. All the lines are updated at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you enter a committed date, the target date takes the same value. You can adjust it before applying the change.

## Change the item or quantities

If you need to change the item or quantities, manufacturing or purchasing may already have started. Use the line reset workflow to stop related work before applying the change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset the line">
    Click **Reset** to stop related work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the data">
    Change the item or quantities and save the changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the line">
    Click **Confirm** again so the line generates demand with the updated data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
