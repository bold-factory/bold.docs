Edit an order to correct its data or reflect a customer change without creating another document.

<Note>
  You can change a customer reference, correct an error or adjust a delivery date.
</Note>

## Edit the order

**Path:** **Planning > Sales orders > Order you want to edit**

From this screen, you can change:

- **Order no.**;
- **Customer order no.**;
- **Customer**;
- **Order date**;
- **Short description**;
- **Delivery instructions**;
- **Notes**.

Click **Save** after editing the header.

## Edit an order line

**Path:** **Planning > Sales orders > Order you want to edit > Item line**

To edit the requested, committed or target delivery dates, open the item line, make your changes and click **Save**.

If you need to change the item or quantities, manufacturing or purchasing may already have started. Use the line reset workflow to stop related work before applying the change.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset the line">
    Click **Reset** to stop related work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the data">
    Change the item or quantities and save the changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the line">
    Click **Confirm** again so the line generates demand with the updated data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
