**Path:** **Production > Sequencing**

Sequence manually when you need to adjust a few orders or operations without creating a production plan. Changes to target dates directly affect pending shop floor work.

## Choose the level of work

The **Production sequencing** screen offers three tabs:

| Tab | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Orders** | To prioritize complete MOs according to their required or target dates. |
| **Operations** | To adjust specific tasks and group operations that will run together at a workstation. |
| **Plans** | To calculate and review a complete scenario before applying it. |

## Orders

Use **Orders** to review all open MOs. Sort by required or target date and change each order's target date when you need to change its priority.

### Sort the list

- **Required date:** sorts by the date when the MO must be completed.

- **Target date:** sorts by the internal date planned to execute shop floor work.

### Filter the list

Filters only change which MOs you see. Each MO's number still shows its position in the full sequence, and the list shows how many MOs are visible out of the total.

- **Hide planned:** hides MOs that already have a target date.
- **Firm dates only:** shows only MOs with a **Firm** required date, whether from manual requests or sales orders with a committed date. See [Manufacturing requests](/en/concepts/manufacturing/manufacturing-requests#required-date-of-a-sales-order-line).
- **Due date:** limits the list by required date.

| Option | MOs shown |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | All of them, with no date filter. |
| **Overdue** | Required date before today. |
| **Today** | Required date today. |
| **Up to 7 days** | Required date within the next 7 days, including overdue ones. |
| **Custom** | Required date between the **From** and **To** dates, both included. You can leave either one empty. |

When you filter by required date, MOs without a required date are hidden. You can combine filters. For example, **Firm dates only** with **Today** shows the deliveries committed for today.

### Adjust the target date

The list shows each MO's calculated priority. Change its **target date** to bring it forward or delay it. Bold shows an alert if the new date is after the required date, together with the days of delay. The delay is shown in red when the required date is firm and in amber when it is not.

## Operations

Use **Operations** for physical tasks such as cutting, milling, or painting. Select an area or workstation and filter by tool if needed.

- Adjust an operation's target date when its priority should differ from the rest of the MO.
- Group operations when they should run together at the same workstation.
- Filter by tool to prepare consecutive operations sharing a mold, fixture, or setup.

## Plans

Use **Plans** to calculate a complete sequence, review restrictions, and adjust the result in a diagram before applying it.
