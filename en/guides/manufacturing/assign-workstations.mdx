On this screen, you can assign the workstation and, where applicable, the tool used in each operation. The view also lets you review workload and identify operations without assigned resources.

**Path:** **Production > Resource assignment**

## Pending operations

Each card represents a pending operation. You can view its main details without opening the MO:

- **Identification:** MO code, such as `OF-00029-01`, and operation name, such as cutting, gluing, or sanding.
- **Priority:** deadline, requester, and completed quantity compared with the required quantity, such as `0/12 pieces`.

## Assign or change machinery

**Workstation:** an operation can be compatible with several workstations. The selector shows only workstations suitable for the process. If you change a workstation after the operation is planned, Bold asks you to confirm how to handle the existing schedule.

**Tool:** appears when the operation requires a specific characteristic. The selector shows compatible tools.

## Filter operations

Use the filters at the top to narrow the list:

- **Area or workstation:** shows only tasks in an area, such as _Painting_, or at a workstation, such as _Belt sander_. This lets you review the queue for a specific section.
- **Quick status filters:** use **Hide planned** and **Hide started** to focus on work that still needs assignment.

### Example: reassign work after a breakdown

If **Hydraulic press 1** will be stopped for two days:

1. Select **Hydraulic press 1** in the **Workstation** filter.
2. Review operations assigned to that machine.
3. Open each card and reassign it to **Hydraulic press 2** or an alternative workbench.
