**Pause reasons** explain why an operation or group of operations stops. They can be planned, such as a break or shift change, or unplanned, such as a breakdown, missing materials, or a quality issue.

Recording the reason lets you analyze losses and recurring stops.

## Steps to follow

**Path:** **Production > Pause reasons > Add new reason**

**Name:** enter a short title that is easy to recognize on the pause screen. For example: `Lunch`, `Scheduled maintenance`, `Shift meeting`, or `Line cleaning`.

**Description (optional):** explain when to use the reason so the whole workshop follows the same criteria. For example: `30-minute break for the morning shift. Use only between 13:00 and 14:00.`

**Interruption type:** choose **Planned** or **Unplanned**. The form explains how each option affects OEE.

- **Planned:** for predictable stops in your usual operations, such as `Lunch`, `Shift change`, or `Scheduled cleaning`. Their duration is subtracted from planned production time, so they do not reduce the workstation's availability or OEE.
- **Unplanned:** for unexpected events such as `Electrical fault`, `Raw material shortage`, or `Machine jam`. Their duration counts as lost production time, so they reduce availability and, with it, OEE.

OEE is calculated as availability × performance × quality, where availability is running time divided by shift time minus planned stops.
