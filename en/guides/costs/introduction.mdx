## Recommended order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review base data">
    Complete item, employee and resource costs before analyzing variances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Perform traceable work">
    Record purchases, consumption, production, time and maintenance work orders with their corresponding references.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare planned and actual costs">
    Analyze differences by order, work order, item or lot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review inventory">
    Check that stock, lots and the costs used in valuation are consistent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before you start

Analysis depends on complete operational data. If consumption, time records, prices or lots are missing, correct them before interpreting the results.
