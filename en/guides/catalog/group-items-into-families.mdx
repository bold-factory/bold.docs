In Bold, a **family** groups items that belong to the same commercial or technical concept. Create one when you want to organize variants under a single family.

<Info>
  If you only have a standalone item and do not need to group variants, you can work directly with the item.
</Info>

## Create a family

**Path:** **Catalog > Families > Create new**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the data">
    **Name:** enter the main, most visible name of what you manufacture or store.

    **Code:** enter a stable, unique alphanumeric identifier for the family.

    **Description (optional):** add family details that do not fit in the name, such as dimensions, materials, colors or storage warnings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the properties">
    Within the created family, click **Require property** and select the properties its items must complete.

    <Note>
      **Example:** if you manufacture T-shirts, you can require **Color** and **Size** to distinguish a red size M T-shirt from a blue size XL T-shirt.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Effect of properties

When you assign a property to a family, all its items must have a value for it. If it is missing, the item is marked as invalid until you complete the information.

You should not have two active items with the same family and property values. If they represent exactly the same physical element, review their related manufacturing orders, requests and recipes before consolidating them. If they differ, a property is missing to explain the difference.
