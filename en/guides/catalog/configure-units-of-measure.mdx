**Units of measure** specify how you quantify each item. Configure them before creating items tracked in kilograms, liters, meters or other units that differ from the default unit.

**Path:** **Catalog > Units of measure**

## Create a unit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start creating the unit">
    Click **Create new**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Enter a short **Code**, such as `kg`, `l` or `m`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the name">
    Enter the **Name** the user will see, such as `Kilograms`, `Liters` or `Meters`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the unit">
    Click **Create**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can then select the unit as the main unit when creating an item, or add it as an alternative unit with its equivalence.

<Note>
  Decimal precision is configured for each item, rather than for the unit of measure.
</Note>
